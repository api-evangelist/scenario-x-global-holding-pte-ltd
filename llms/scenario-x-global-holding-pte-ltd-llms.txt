# Scenario X

> Scenario X (SCENARIO-X GLOBAL HOLDING PTE. LTD.) is a financial-technology company building an AI- and quantum-computing-powered platform for financial risk analysis, stress testing, and regulatory reporting. Its integrated platform serves banks, insurers, family offices, independent wealth managers, and commodity traders. As of this writing the company publishes no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable API specification; this profile tracks the company as an API Evangelist network lead.

## Products
- [Scenario Module](https://scenario-x.ai/scenario/): Designs and analyzes adverse market conditions for risk preparation.
- [PPNR Module](https://scenario-x.ai/ppnr/): Pre-Provision Net Revenue projections and regulatory compliance.
- [Risk Module](https://scenario-x.ai/risk/): Market, counterparty, credit, and operational risk assessment.
- [Console Module](https://scenario-x.ai/console/): Aggregates financial data for streamlined analysis.
